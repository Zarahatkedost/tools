Phone formatter

Turn any number into the international format, a click-to-call link, and a WhatsApp link.

What this does. Pick the country, type the local number in any messy format — 5200 0831, 052000831, (52) 000-831 — and get the clean E.164 international format (+97452000831) that CRMs, WhatsApp, and dialers expect. It strips spaces and punctuation, drops the local leading zero where countries use one, and warns if the length looks wrong for that country. You also get a click-to-call tel: link and a ready wa.me WhatsApp link.
