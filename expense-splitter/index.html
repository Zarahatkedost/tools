Expense splitter

Split a bill with tax and tip — evenly, or with different shares per person.

What this does. Enter the bill, tax % and tip %, add the people, and it shows exactly what each person owes. By default everyone pays equally; change a person's share weight to make them pay more or less (weight 2 = pays double a weight-1 person; 0.5 = pays half). Tip can be calculated on the pre-tax or post-tax amount — restaurants differ, so it's your choice.
