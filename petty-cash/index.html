Petty cash tracker

Log expenses, track the remaining balance, and export to Excel or PDF.

What this does. Record each petty-cash expense — date, what it was for, amount, who paid, who received it, invoice number, and category. It keeps a running total of what's been spent and how much of your starting cash float remains. When you're done, export a CSV (opens in Excel) or use Print to save a PDF. Important: entries live in this browser only and are not saved automatically — export before you close the tab, and re-import isn't supported, so treat the exported file as your record.
