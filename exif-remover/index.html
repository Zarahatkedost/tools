EXIF remover

Strip hidden metadata — GPS location, camera details, timestamps — from photos before sharing.

What this does. Photos carry invisible metadata (EXIF): often the exact GPS location where they were taken, the device model, and timestamps. This tool redraws each image onto a clean canvas and re-exports it — which by nature discards all metadata — right on your device; nothing is uploaded. Choose JPEG (small files) or PNG (pixel-perfect). Download images one by one, or all together as a zip. Note: because the image is re-encoded, file size may change slightly.
