Email validator

Paste a list of emails — get them checked, deduplicated, and cleaned for your campaign.

What this does. Checks every address for correct format, catches common typos in big domains (gmial.com → gmail.com, hotmial → hotmail…), flags disposable-mail domains, and removes duplicates. Then you can copy or download just the clean list. Honest limit: this checks format only — no tool can confirm a mailbox actually exists without sending to it. Perfect for cleaning a list before a mail merge so obvious junk doesn't bounce.
