Shipping calculator

Work out shipment volume, chargeable weight, and an estimated landed cost.

What this does. Add each box in your shipment (length, width, height, quantity, actual weight). The tool works out the total volume (CBM), the volumetric weight (size converted to a billed weight), and the chargeable weight — the higher of actual vs. volumetric, which is what carriers actually bill. Then it estimates a landed cost from goods value, customs duty, your margin, and government fees. The size & weight maths are exact and work anywhere; the cost figures are estimates you type in and can edit freely.

Qatar note. Detailed-mode defaults reflect typical Qatar sea-freight paperwork: port clearance ~450 QAR (sea) / ~750 QAR (air), legalization ~1000, chamber stamp ~800, plus other fees — landing roughly 2,800–3,800 QAR for sea. Customs duty is commonly 5% of value for most goods. Logistics providers usually bundle port clearance into their charge. Other countries differ — edit every field to match your route.
