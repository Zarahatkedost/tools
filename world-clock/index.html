World clock

Live times around the world, UTC pinned on top, plus a meeting planner to find overlapping hours.

What this does. Shows the current time in as many cities as you like, updating every second. UTC is always pinned at the top as the reference. Add or remove cities freely. The meeting planner below lets you slide a proposed hour and instantly see what time that is everywhere — with a colour showing whether it lands in working hours, waking hours, or the middle of the night. All times are computed with your browser's built-in timezone database, so daylight-saving is handled correctly.
Reference UTC — Coordinated Universal Time
